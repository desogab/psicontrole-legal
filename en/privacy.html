Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

Overview

PsiControle is designed as a local-first clinical organization app. This privacy policy explains how the app handles data and what information is collected or processed.

Local Storage by Default

The app may store patient information, appointments and recurrence data, documents, addresses, notes, reminders, receipts, financial status, settings, and other administrative data created by the user.

Versions earlier than 1.0.8 do not offer CloudKit sync for PsiControle app data; this data is stored locally on the device. When you update from one of those versions to 1.0.8 or later, your existing data is preserved and remains local until you choose to enable iCloud sync. Updating alone does not send your data to iCloud. The app does not send this data to servers controlled by the developer.

Optional iCloud and CloudKit Sync

Starting with version 1.0.8, PsiControle offers optional iCloud sync. Users can enable sync in Settings → iCloud and storage after reading and confirming the consent. Enabling or disabling sync inside the app takes effect after fully quitting and reopening the app.

When enabled, a copy of patients, appointments, recurrence data, documents, addresses, notes, reminders, and financial records is sent to Apple services, in the private CloudKit database associated with the device’s iCloud account. Existing records are included. Other devices running a compatible app version with the same iCloud account can access this data; the app does not publish it in the public database or share it between accounts.

This private database belongs to your iCloud account and stores PsiControle data for your use. The development team and others responsible for PsiControle do not have access to its contents and cannot view them through CloudKit administration tools. Access through the app occurs on your devices signed in to the same iCloud account; other PsiControle users cannot access your records.

Settings preferences, system permissions, and device authentication remain local. The App Store account used for subscriptions does not replace the iCloud account used for data.

Sync depends on the account, connection, available iCloud storage, and system scheduling. Changes may remain pending while the device is offline. Sync is not an independent backup: edits and deletions can also propagate.

Data Protection

On the device, the app uses iOS file protection. In CloudKit, the content fields of synced models are configured as encrypted fields. Record identifiers, relationships, and other metadata required to operate the service do not have the same classification as content fields.

iCloud data is encrypted in transit and on servers. With Advanced Data Protection enabled for the Apple account, CloudKit encrypted fields and assets receive end-to-end protection. This setting is not required to enable sync; the app does not check or enable it. Read Apple’s iCloud security description.

Sync Controls and Apple Account

Disabling sync inside the app stops CloudKit use on the next process launch and does not, by itself, delete local records or the copy already sent to iCloud. Enabling sync again does not undo deletions: changes made in the same local database while sync was off, including deletions, may be sent to iCloud.

Signing out of the Apple account, switching accounts, or disabling the app’s iCloud access in iOS Settings behaves differently. The native mechanism may remove the local replica, including changes not yet uploaded. Export data you need to keep before these changes.

Import and Export

CSV import, when used, runs locally on the device and may include registration data such as name, contact information, status, notes, document information, and address information.

Users may also export data as CSV or in other formats made available by the app. Files are created locally and sent only to the destination selected by the user through iOS sharing and storage options. After export, the selected destination or service controls how the file is handled.

Calendar and Notifications

Calendar sync is optional. Full Calendar access is used only to create, update, and remove PsiControle appointment events in a dedicated or user-selected calendar.

The app does not import, store, or display the user's personal calendar events.

By default, calendar events and notifications use neutral text without patient name, notes, phone, email, document, or appointment type.

Notifications are used only for appointment reminders or app-related reminders configured by the user.

Siri, Shortcuts, and Spotlight

PsiControle provides optional actions through Siri, Shortcuts, and App Intents to review or perform tasks such as checking the schedule, creating or completing appointments, and recording payments.

These actions are processed through Apple system features and may display or receive the data needed to perform the command. Actions that read or change clinical data require device authentication as configured by the app and iOS.

Patient indexing in Spotlight and Siri is optional and can be disabled in PsiControle Settings. When enabled, information needed for search, such as the patient's name and record identifier, is provided to the device's local system index.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple using StoreKit. Payment information is handled by Apple, not by PsiControle.

The app only checks the App Store entitlement that grants access.

Collection and Tracking

The app does not include an analytics SDK.

The app does not track users across apps and websites.

The app does not share data with third parties for advertising, analytics, or sale. Optional features initiated by the user may provide data to iOS and the Apple services required for Calendar, notifications, Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, subscriptions, iCloud and CloudKit sync, backups, or export.

The app does not sell user data.

Apple Analytics and App Store Connect

Apple may provide the developer with aggregate App Store Connect metrics, such as downloads, sessions, subscriptions, and crashes, depending on the user's Apple analytics sharing settings.

Backups and Exported Files

iCloud device backups are managed by iOS according to the user’s Apple account settings. They are separate from CloudKit app sync. The app does not confirm that a device backup exists, is intact, or can be restored.

Exported files, shared copies, and earlier backups are not erased by deletion inside the app. Users control their retention and removal at the selected destinations.

Data Sharing

PsiControle does not send patient, appointment, document, address, or note data to servers controlled by the developer. Enabling sync authorizes storage in Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit services. Other optional integrations and exports provide data to services or destinations selected by the user.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains in local storage and, when sync is used, in the iCloud account until users delete it or system mechanisms remove it. The app does not set an automatic expiration period for clinical and financial records.

Users can delete records inside the app. With sync active, deletions of records present on this device are forwarded to CloudKit when the system can process them and may affect other synced copies. Local deletion confirmation does not prove that the entire remote database has been erased: records not yet imported may remain. The app does not provide an action to erase the entire private remote database.

Disabling sync or removing the app does not automatically delete the CloudKit copy. Removing the app deletes its local storage, but not exported files, backups, or external copies. Deleting data or removing the app does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancellation is managed in the Apple account’s subscription settings.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for professional use and is not directed to children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
